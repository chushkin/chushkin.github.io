Privacy Policy

This policy describes the iOS version of Glamour Girls: Jigsaw Puzzle.

Game data

The game does not require an account. Your progress, settings and favorites are stored locally on your device. The current iOS version does not include advertising or analytics and does not send your game progress to the developer's server. Your device's backup settings may include app data.

Downloads

You can choose to download an illustration using the option provided in the game. The game does not browse your photo library.

Deleting data

You can remove locally stored game data by deleting the app. Device backups and any files you saved separately are managed using your device's settings and apps.

Contacting support

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to provide and uses it to answer your request. You may request deletion of your correspondence by contacting the same address.

Contact

Developer: Eugene Chushkin. For support and privacy questions: eugene.chushkin@gmail.com.
