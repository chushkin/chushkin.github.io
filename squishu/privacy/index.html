Squishu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Squishu ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game developed by Eugene Chushkin. This policy describes the current version, which is free and has no advertising or in-app purchases for real money.

What we collect

The game does not collect personal data or send gameplay data to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. You can play offline.

Coins are earned in the game. Capsules and other items use in-game coins only; they cannot be bought for real money, exchanged for money or cashed out.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, pet, collection and settings) is saved in local app storage. The game does not provide its own cloud account or cloud synchronization service.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups or device-to-device transfers, depending on your device, operating system and backup settings. Those services are controlled by your operating system provider, not by the game developer. The developer does not receive your device backups.

You can erase the game's current local progress in Settings → Reset progress. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local app data, but an operating system backup or device transfer may restore it. Manage or delete system backups through your device or backup provider's settings. Resetting progress in the game does not delete existing operating system backups.

Device features

  • Vibration / haptics — to give soft feedback while you play. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Motion sensor — only to detect when you shake the phone for the Shake booster. Motion data is processed on the device in real time and is never stored or sent anywhere. On iPhone the system asks your permission first.
  • Share — when you tap Share, the game opens your device's share sheet with a short text (for example, your daily result). You choose whether and where to share it. Any service you choose handles the shared text under its own privacy policy.

Support and external pages

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message and any attachments. We use it to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information. You may ask us to delete your support correspondence by contacting the address below; legal retention requirements may apply.

Opening a developer website, app store page or another external service is separate from playing offline. Those services may process requests and technical information under their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example, online leaderboards or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
