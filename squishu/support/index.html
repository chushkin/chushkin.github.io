Reporting a problem

Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, the game version if available, the mode or level, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help. Avoid sending passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal details.

Availability and payments

Squishu is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store. The Android version is in preparation. The current game has no advertising or in-app purchases for real money. Coins, boosters and capsules use in-game resources only and cannot be cashed out.

Offline play and progress

You do not need an account or an internet connection to play. Progress, including levels, coins, your pet, collection and settings, stays in local app storage. The game does not provide its own cloud account or cloud synchronization service.

Your operating system may include app data in backups or device transfers, depending on its settings. The developer cannot retrieve a lost save from a server. Do not uninstall the game as your first troubleshooting step if you want to keep your progress.

Sound, haptics and effects

Open Settings to turn sounds, music or haptics on or off. Calm mode reduces screen shake and visual effects; it does not remove the gameplay tricks in Adventure. Surprise events in Classic have a separate switch.

The Shake booster also has an on-screen button. You do not need to physically shake your phone to activate it.

Learning the rules

Open How to play from Settings, or try the short Squish Lab boards. Fill a complete row, column or outlined 3×3 box to clear it. Three matching animals in a line pop, and bigger matches can leave power-ups.

Resetting progress

Use Settings → Reset progress to erase the game's current local progress. This removes levels, coins, collection and pet progress. It does not delete existing operating system backups. Manage those through your device or backup provider's settings.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request and investigate the issue. Support correspondence is stored in Gmail until manually deleted. You can request deletion by emailing the address above; legal retention requirements may apply.

For details about the game, device features, backups and external services, read the privacy policy.
