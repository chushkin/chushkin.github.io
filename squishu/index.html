Find your way to play

A tiny world.
Plenty to explore.

Travel through 32 worlds, hatch a pet, and collect 24 little figures using resources earned in the game. No real-money purchases.

Rotate pieces with spin tokens, save one in your Pocket, and discover new obstacles as you go. Short Squish Lab boards introduce the mechanics.

  • Adventure320 levels with different goals and occasional visits from Impy.
  • ClassicAn endless run to chase your best score.

Before your first game

A few answers.

Visit support ↗
Can I download Squishu now?

Yes! Squishu is available for iPhone, iPad and Android. Download it on the App Store or get it on Google Play.

Are there ads or real-money purchases?

The current game is free and contains no ads or in-app purchases. Coins and capsules are in-game resources; they cannot be purchased for real money or cashed out.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is required to play, and the game works offline. Progress is stored on your device. Squishu does not provide its own cloud account or cloud synchronization service.

Who is the game for?

Squishu is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over who enjoy colorful block puzzles. It is not directed at children under 13.
