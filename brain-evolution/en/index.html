01 / 04 · Numbers

Find your rhythm.

One calculation, one sequence, one next goal. Start with challenges where numbers do the talking.

  • Calculator

    Work through arithmetic problems and enter your answer.

  • Numbers

    Find numbers on the board in ascending order.

02 / 04 · Memory

Look. Remember. Play.

Notice the details, keep them in mind and put them to the test with your next move.

  • Memorics

    Remember shapes and their features.

  • Pairs

    Turn over cards and find matching pairs.

  • Geometry

    Remember an outline and recreate it by connecting points.

03 / 04 · Logic

Make every move count.

Plan a route, find the right number or change just one detail. There is always another way to try.

  • Sudoku

    Fill the 9 × 9 grid with notes and candidates to help you.

  • Matches

    Solve matchstick puzzles.

  • Minesweeper

    Reveal cells, flag mines and read the clues.

  • Balltracker

    Plan a ball’s route through an obstacle-filled board to the exit.

04 / 04 · Observation

It is all in the details.

Among colours, arrows and similar-looking objects, find what matters for your next move.

  • Quadronica

    Find rectangles with four matching corner tiles.

  • Arrow Path

    Follow the direction of the central arrow.

  • GuessWho

    Recognise an object from its distinguishing features.

  • Encyclopedia

    Remember facts and answer questions.

Before your first game

Support
Which version is shown on this page?+

The gallery previews the upcoming version 1.2.0. On October 25, 2026, the App Store offers version 1.0.7 and RuStore offers version 1.1.8. Update 1.2.0 has not been published yet. Check the version available in the store before downloading.

Which devices can I use?+

The current version is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store, and for Android on RuStore. The buttons on this page link to the official app listings.

How do profiles and progress work in 1.2.0?+

The upcoming version lets players create local profiles and keep results separate. Stars, medals and ranks relate to progress through the games.

Which languages are available in 1.2.0?+

Settings offer English, Russian, Ukrainian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Turkish and Hebrew. Some new panels are still displayed in English or Russian.
